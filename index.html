Humans, technology
& and its impact.

This is a place to reflect on how the tools we create affect and influence our lives, as well as the way we live and interact.

This website wil be the home of my Human Artifact for the duration of this course and will serve as a space for my thoughts and insights.

01 / About me

Hi, I'm Ronaldo.

I'm a Biochemistry student at Florida International University with an interest in medicine, science, and the ways technology affects people and the importance of their development.

I want to look beyond the tools themselves and delve more into how they shape the world around us and our everyday lives as individuals and also as a collective human race.

02 / Purpose

Whats the point of this website?

I'm developing this site to connect the learnings from my course and personal research with the real world impacts of technology on our lives and the world.

My goal is to reflect on how these tools came to be, their historical context, and the potential consequesnces of these on the world, wether negative or positve.
